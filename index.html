Speaking Helper Cards

1
The Presenter

Hello everyone! In our group, we focused on the problem of endless scrolling. That's why we designed FocusPal. Our slogan is "Less scrolling, more knowing."

2
The Developer

Our app has two main features. First, it will track how many minutes you spend on each app automatically when you start revising. Second, it is going to send you a gentle reminder after fifteen minutes of scrolling so that you can keep track of your daily study goals.

3
The Study Coach

Here is our advice for exam week: students should put their phone in another room while studying every day. Most importantly, you had better not check your phone every time it buzzes, or you will fail to prepare well!
